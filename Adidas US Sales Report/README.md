Adidas US Sales Performance Analysis – Power BI Project

Project Description
This project focuses on analyzing Adidas US sales data to uncover meaningful business insights using Power BI. The objective was to transform raw transactional data into interactive dashboards that help evaluate overall sales performance, product demand, customer purchasing behavior, and regional profitability.
The project simulates a real-world business intelligence scenario where data is cleaned, modeled, and visualized to support strategic decision-making. Three interactive dashboards were designed to provide a complete view of business performance, enabling stakeholders to quickly identify growth opportunities and operational improvements.

Key Challenges Faced
During this project, several practical challenges were encountered similar to real-world data analytics workflows:
Cleaning and standardizing raw sales data to ensure consistency and accuracy
Handling time-based analysis using dynamic date intelligence calculations
Managing DAX filter context while creating KPI and ranking measures
Designing dashboards that balance visual appeal with business storytelling
Optimizing performance while maintaining interactivity across multiple visuals

Skills Gained
Data Cleaning & Transformation using Power Query
Advanced DAX for KPI calculations and performance analysis
Data Modeling and Time Intelligence Implementation
Dashboard Design and Business Storytelling
Analytical Thinking and Problem-Solving
Creating Professional and Interactive Business Reports

Project Outcome
The final dashboards provide clear insights into revenue trends, top-performing products, customer demand patterns, and regional sales performance. The project demonstrates the ability to convert complex business data into actionable insights using modern Business Intelligence tools.
