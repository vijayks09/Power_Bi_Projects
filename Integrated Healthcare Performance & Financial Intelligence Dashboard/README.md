Healthcare Analytics Dashboard | Power BI Project

Developed an end-to-end healthcare analytics solution using Power BI to analyze 55,000+ patient records and deliver actionable insights into hospital operations, clinical trends, and financial performance.

Project Overview:
Built 3 interactive dashboards covering Hospital Performance, Clinical Insights, and Financial & Insurance Analysis
Designed 15+ KPIs including Patient Volume, Revenue Performance, Length of Stay, and Revenue per Patient
Applied Power Query for data cleaning, transformation, and segmentation (Age Groups, Billing Categories)
Created advanced DAX measures for dynamic business insights and performance tracking
Implemented executive-friendly visualization layouts for decision support

Key Business Insights:
Identified correlation between emergency admissions and increased hospital stay duration
Highlighted high-revenue medical conditions and major insurance contributors
Provided patient demographic and treatment complexity analysis

Challenges Faced:
Standardizing inconsistent medical and hospital data formats
Selecting meaningful KPIs aligned with business goals
Choosing appropriate visuals to improve data storytelling clarity
Balancing dashboard detail while maintaining usability
